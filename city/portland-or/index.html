Cost of Living Overview

Portland has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 138 (where 100 equals the national median), Portland residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,596/month while earning a median household income of $88,792/year.

The median home value in Portland, OR is $557,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,771 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.6% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Oregon's top income tax rate of 9.9% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 0% sales tax rate and 0.93% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Portland.

What is the cost of living in Portland, OR?
