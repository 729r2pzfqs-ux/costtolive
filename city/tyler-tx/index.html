Cost of Living Overview

Tyler is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 79 (where 100 equals the national median), Tyler residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,199/month while earning a median household income of $65,527/year.

The median home value in Tyler, TX is $216,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,170 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.0% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Tyler.

What is the cost of living in Tyler, TX?

Is Tyler, TX affordable?
