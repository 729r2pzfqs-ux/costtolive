Cost of Living Overview

Berkeley has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 251 (where 100 equals the national median), Berkeley residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,127/month while earning a median household income of $108,558/year.

The median home value in Berkeley, CA is $1,351,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,340 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 23.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Berkeley.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Berkeley

What is the cost of living in Berkeley, CA?
The cost of living index in Berkeley, CA is 251 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $2,127, median household income is $108,558/year, and the median home value is $1,351,100.
Is Berkeley, CA affordable?
No, Berkeley is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 251. The rent-to-income ratio is 23.5%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Berkeley, CA?

What is the median household income in Berkeley?
The median household income in Berkeley, CA is $108,558 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in California?
