Cost of Living Overview

Sparks has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 126 (where 100 equals the national median), Sparks residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,651/month while earning a median household income of $86,979/year.

The median home value in Sparks, NV is $455,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,610 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Nevada has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.85% sales tax rate and 0.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Sparks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Sparks

What is the cost of living in Sparks, NV?
The cost of living index in Sparks, NV is 126 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,651, median household income is $86,979/year, and the median home value is $455,900.
Is Sparks, NV affordable?
No, Sparks is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 126. The rent-to-income ratio is 22.8%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Sparks, NV?

What is the median household income in Sparks?

What are the taxes like in Nevada?
