Cost of Living Overview

Westminster has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 139 (where 100 equals the national median), Westminster residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,816/month while earning a median household income of $96,145/year.

The median home value in Westminster, CO is $497,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,841 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.7% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Colorado has a moderate income tax rate of 4.4%. Combined with a 2.9% sales tax rate and 0.51% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Westminster.
