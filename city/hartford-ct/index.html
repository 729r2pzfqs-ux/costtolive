Cost of Living Overview

Hartford is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 81 (where 100 equals the national median), Hartford residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,221/month while earning a median household income of $45,300/year.

The median home value in Hartford, CT is $217,200, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,279 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. At 32.3% rent-to-income ratio, residents of Hartford are spending more than the recommended 30% of income on housing, indicating moderate cost burden.

Connecticut has a moderate income tax rate of 6.99%. Combined with a 6.35% sales tax rate and 2.15% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Hartford.
