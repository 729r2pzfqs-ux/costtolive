Cost of Living Overview

Lehigh Acres has a cost of living close to the national average, offering a balanced mix of expenses and amenities. With a cost index of 95 (where 100 equals the national median), Lehigh Acres residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,496/month while earning a median household income of $62,330/year.

The median home value in Lehigh Acres, FL is $263,200, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,330 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 28.8%, Lehigh Acres falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Lehigh Acres.
