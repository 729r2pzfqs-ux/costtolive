Cost of Living Overview

Tampa is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 113 (where 100 equals the national median), Tampa residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,567/month while earning a median household income of $71,302/year.

The median home value in Tampa, FL is $375,300, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,553 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.4%, Tampa falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Tampa.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Tampa

What is the cost of living in Tampa, FL?
The cost of living index in Tampa, FL is 113 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,567, median household income is $71,302/year, and the median home value is $375,300.
Is Tampa, FL affordable?
No, Tampa is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 113. The rent-to-income ratio is 26.4%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Tampa, FL?
The median gross rent in Tampa, FL is $1,567 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $18,804.
What is the median household income in Tampa?
The median household income in Tampa, FL is $71,302 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Florida?
Florida has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.0%, and an effective property tax rate of 0.86%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
