Cost of Living Overview

Odessa is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 83 (where 100 equals the national median), Odessa residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,322/month while earning a median household income of $73,030/year.

The median home value in Odessa, TX is $208,000, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,251 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.7% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Odessa.

What is the cost of living in Odessa, TX?
The cost of living index in Odessa, TX is 83 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,322, median household income is $73,030/year, and the median home value is $208,000.
Is Odessa, TX affordable?
Yes, Odessa is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 83. The rent-to-income ratio is 21.7%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.

What is the median household income in Odessa?
