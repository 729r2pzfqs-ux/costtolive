Cost of Living Overview

Brownsville is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 54 (where 100 equals the national median), Brownsville residents can expect to pay median rent of $906/month while earning a median household income of $48,675/year.

The median home value in Brownsville, TX is $122,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $830 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Brownsville.
