Cost of Living Overview

Thornton has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 138 (where 100 equals the national median), Thornton residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,810/month while earning a median household income of $100,985/year.

The median home value in Thornton, CO is $482,200, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,914 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Colorado has a moderate income tax rate of 4.4%. Combined with a 2.9% sales tax rate and 0.51% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Thornton.
