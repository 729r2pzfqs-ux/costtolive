Cost of Living Overview

Tulsa is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 67 (where 100 equals the national median), Tulsa residents can expect to pay median rent of $998/month while earning a median household income of $58,407/year.

The median home value in Tulsa, OK is $189,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,027 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Oklahoma has a moderate income tax rate of 4.75%. Combined with a 4.5% sales tax rate and 0.9% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Tulsa.
