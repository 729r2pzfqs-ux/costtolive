Cost of Living Overview

Fresno has a cost of living close to the national average, offering a balanced mix of expenses and amenities. With a cost index of 101 (where 100 equals the national median), Fresno residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,324/month while earning a median household income of $66,804/year.

The median home value in Fresno, CA is $348,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,391 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 23.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Fresno.

Common questions about living costs in Fresno

What is the cost of living in Fresno, CA?
The cost of living index in Fresno, CA is 101 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,324, median household income is $66,804/year, and the median home value is $348,500.
Is Fresno, CA affordable?
No, Fresno is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 101. The rent-to-income ratio is 23.8%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
