Cost of Living Overview

Shreveport is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 65 (where 100 equals the national median), Shreveport residents can expect to pay median rent of $980/month while earning a median household income of $48,465/year.

The median home value in Shreveport, LA is $178,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $984 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Louisiana has a moderate income tax rate of 4.25%. Combined with a 4.45% sales tax rate and 0.55% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Shreveport.
