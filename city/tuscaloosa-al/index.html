Cost of Living Overview

Tuscaloosa is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 73 (where 100 equals the national median), Tuscaloosa residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,005/month while earning a median household income of $48,536/year.

The median home value in Tuscaloosa, AL is $236,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,038 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Alabama has a moderate income tax rate of 5.0%. Combined with a 4.0% sales tax rate and 0.41% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Tuscaloosa.
