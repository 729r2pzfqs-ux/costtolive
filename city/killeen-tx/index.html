Cost of Living Overview

Killeen is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 75 (where 100 equals the national median), Killeen residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,147/month while earning a median household income of $58,339/year.

The median home value in Killeen, TX is $196,000, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,176 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 23.6% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Killeen.
