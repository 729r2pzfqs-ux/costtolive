Cost of Living Overview

Meridian has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 131 (where 100 equals the national median), Meridian residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,714/month while earning a median household income of $98,686/year.

The median home value in Meridian, ID is $485,000, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,607 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Idaho has a moderate income tax rate of 5.8%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.63% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Meridian.
