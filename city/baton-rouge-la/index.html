Cost of Living Overview

Baton Rouge is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 73 (where 100 equals the national median), Baton Rouge residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,044/month while earning a median household income of $49,944/year.

The median home value in Baton Rouge, LA is $224,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,043 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.1%, Baton Rouge falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Louisiana has a moderate income tax rate of 4.25%. Combined with a 4.45% sales tax rate and 0.55% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Baton Rouge.
