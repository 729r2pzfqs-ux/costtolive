Cost of Living Overview

League City is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 114 (where 100 equals the national median), League City residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,628/month while earning a median household income of $119,870/year.

The median home value in League City, TX is $334,000, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,766 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 16.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in League City.

What is the cost of living in League City, TX?
