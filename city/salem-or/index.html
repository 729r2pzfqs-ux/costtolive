Cost of Living Overview

Salem is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 105 (where 100 equals the national median), Salem residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,323/month while earning a median household income of $71,900/year.

The median home value in Salem, OR is $382,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,440 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Oregon's top income tax rate of 9.9% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 0% sales tax rate and 0.93% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Salem.
