Cost of Living Overview

Coral Springs has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 146 (where 100 equals the national median), Coral Springs residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,951/month while earning a median household income of $90,643/year.

The median home value in Coral Springs, FL is $502,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,969 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.8%, Coral Springs falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Coral Springs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Coral Springs

What is the cost of living in Coral Springs, FL?
The cost of living index in Coral Springs, FL is 146 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,951, median household income is $90,643/year, and the median home value is $502,700.
Is Coral Springs, FL affordable?
No, Coral Springs is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 146. The rent-to-income ratio is 25.8%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Coral Springs, FL?

What is the median household income in Coral Springs?
The median household income in Coral Springs, FL is $90,643 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Florida?
Florida has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.0%, and an effective property tax rate of 0.86%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
