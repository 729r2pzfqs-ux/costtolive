Cost of Living Overview

Arvada has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 152 (where 100 equals the national median), Arvada residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,802/month while earning a median household income of $113,396/year.

The median home value in Arvada, CO is $600,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,948 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 19.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Colorado has a moderate income tax rate of 4.4%. Combined with a 2.9% sales tax rate and 0.51% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Arvada.
