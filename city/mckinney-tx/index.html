Cost of Living Overview

McKinney has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 138 (where 100 equals the national median), McKinney residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,841/month while earning a median household income of $120,273/year.

The median home value in McKinney, TX is $439,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,101 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 18.4% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in McKinney.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in McKinney

What is the cost of living in McKinney, TX?
The cost of living index in McKinney, TX is 138 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,841, median household income is $120,273/year, and the median home value is $439,500.
Is McKinney, TX affordable?
No, McKinney is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 138. The rent-to-income ratio is 18.4%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in McKinney, TX?
The median gross rent in McKinney, TX is $1,841 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $22,092.
What is the median household income in McKinney?
The median household income in McKinney, TX is $120,273 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Texas?
Texas has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.6%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
