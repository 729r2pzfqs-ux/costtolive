Cost of Living Overview

Pittsburgh is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 76 (where 100 equals the national median), Pittsburgh residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,221/month while earning a median household income of $64,137/year.

The median home value in Pittsburgh, PA is $193,200, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,136 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Pennsylvania has a moderate income tax rate of 3.07%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 1.49% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Pittsburgh.
