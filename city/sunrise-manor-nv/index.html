Cost of Living Overview

Sunrise Manor offers a below-average cost of living, giving residents more purchasing power compared to many US cities. With a cost index of 90 (where 100 equals the national median), Sunrise Manor residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,284/month while earning a median household income of $55,034/year.

The median home value in Sunrise Manor, NV is $290,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,215 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 28.0%, Sunrise Manor falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Nevada has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.85% sales tax rate and 0.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Sunrise Manor.
