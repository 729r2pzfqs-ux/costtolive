Cost of Living Overview

High Point is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 73 (where 100 equals the national median), High Point residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,062/month while earning a median household income of $61,228/year.

The median home value in High Point, NC is $212,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,084 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

North Carolina has a moderate income tax rate of 4.5%. Combined with a 4.75% sales tax rate and 0.8% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in High Point.
