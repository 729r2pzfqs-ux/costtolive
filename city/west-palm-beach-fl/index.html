Cost of Living Overview

West Palm Beach is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 119 (where 100 equals the national median), West Palm Beach residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,709/month while earning a median household income of $69,261/year.

The median home value in West Palm Beach, FL is $369,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,667 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 29.6%, West Palm Beach falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in West Palm Beach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in West Palm Beach

What is the cost of living in West Palm Beach, FL?
The cost of living index in West Palm Beach, FL is 119 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,709, median household income is $69,261/year, and the median home value is $369,800.
Is West Palm Beach, FL affordable?
No, West Palm Beach is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 119. The rent-to-income ratio is 29.6%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in West Palm Beach, FL?
The median gross rent in West Palm Beach, FL is $1,709 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $20,508.
What is the median household income in West Palm Beach?
The median household income in West Palm Beach, FL is $69,261 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Florida?
Florida has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.0%, and an effective property tax rate of 0.86%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
