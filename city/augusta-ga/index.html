Cost of Living Overview

Augusta is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 67 (where 100 equals the national median), Augusta residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,087/month while earning a median household income of $53,134/year.

The median home value in Augusta, GA is $162,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,053 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Georgia has a moderate income tax rate of 5.49%. Combined with a 4.0% sales tax rate and 0.92% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Augusta.
