Cost of Living Overview

St. Paul offers a below-average cost of living, giving residents more purchasing power compared to many US cities. With a cost index of 91 (where 100 equals the national median), St. Paul residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,248/month while earning a median household income of $73,055/year.

The median home value in St. Paul, MN is $280,300, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,390 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Minnesota's top income tax rate of 9.85% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.875% sales tax rate and 1.11% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in St. Paul.

Common questions about living costs in St. Paul

What is the cost of living in St. Paul, MN?
The cost of living index in St. Paul, MN is 91 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,248, median household income is $73,055/year, and the median home value is $280,300.
Is St. Paul, MN affordable?
Yes, St. Paul is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 91. The rent-to-income ratio is 20.5%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in St. Paul, MN?

What is the median household income in St. Paul?
