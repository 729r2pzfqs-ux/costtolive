Cost of Living Overview

Corona has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 173 (where 100 equals the national median), Corona residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,136/month while earning a median household income of $106,438/year.

The median home value in Corona, CA is $656,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,280 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Corona.
