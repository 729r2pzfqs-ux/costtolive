Cost of Living Overview

Enterprise has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 133 (where 100 equals the national median), Enterprise residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,823/month while earning a median household income of $93,980/year.

The median home value in Enterprise, NV is $449,300, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,823 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 23.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Nevada has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.85% sales tax rate and 0.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Enterprise.
