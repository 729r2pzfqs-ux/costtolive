Cost of Living Overview

Winston-Salem is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 71 (where 100 equals the national median), Winston-Salem residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,033/month while earning a median household income of $57,673/year.

The median home value in Winston-Salem, NC is $208,200, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,040 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

North Carolina has a moderate income tax rate of 4.5%. Combined with a 4.75% sales tax rate and 0.8% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Winston-Salem.
