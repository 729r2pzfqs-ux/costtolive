Cost of Living Overview

Chandler has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 134 (where 100 equals the national median), Chandler residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,806/month while earning a median household income of $103,691/year.

The median home value in Chandler, AZ is $469,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,743 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.9% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Arizona has a moderate income tax rate of 2.5%. Combined with a 5.6% sales tax rate and 0.62% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Chandler.
