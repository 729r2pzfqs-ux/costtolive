Cost of Living Overview

Pembroke Pines has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 130 (where 100 equals the national median), Pembroke Pines residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,910/month while earning a median household income of $81,675/year.

The median home value in Pembroke Pines, FL is $411,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,717 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 28.1%, Pembroke Pines falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Pembroke Pines.
