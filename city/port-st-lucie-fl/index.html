Cost of Living Overview

Port St. Lucie is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 113 (where 100 equals the national median), Port St. Lucie residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,781/month while earning a median household income of $78,137/year.

The median home value in Port St. Lucie, FL is $327,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,507 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 27.4%, Port St. Lucie falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Port St. Lucie.

Common questions about living costs in Port St. Lucie

What is the cost of living in Port St. Lucie, FL?
The cost of living index in Port St. Lucie, FL is 113 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,781, median household income is $78,137/year, and the median home value is $327,700.
Is Port St. Lucie, FL affordable?
No, Port St. Lucie is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 113. The rent-to-income ratio is 27.4%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.

What is the median household income in Port St. Lucie?

What are the taxes like in Florida?
