Cost of Living Overview

Colorado Springs is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 119 (where 100 equals the national median), Colorado Springs residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,562/month while earning a median household income of $83,198/year.

The median home value in Colorado Springs, CO is $420,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,592 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Colorado has a moderate income tax rate of 4.4%. Combined with a 2.9% sales tax rate and 0.51% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Colorado Springs.

Common questions about living costs in Colorado Springs

What is the cost of living in Colorado Springs, CO?
The cost of living index in Colorado Springs, CO is 119 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,562, median household income is $83,198/year, and the median home value is $420,700.
Is Colorado Springs, CO affordable?
No, Colorado Springs is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 119. The rent-to-income ratio is 22.5%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Colorado Springs, CO?

What is the median household income in Colorado Springs?

What are the taxes like in Colorado?
