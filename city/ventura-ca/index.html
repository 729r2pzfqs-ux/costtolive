Cost of Living Overview

Ventura has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 183 (where 100 equals the national median), Ventura residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,101/month while earning a median household income of $100,407/year.

The median home value in Ventura, CA is $778,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,118 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.1%, Ventura falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Ventura.

Common questions about living costs in Ventura

What is the cost of living in Ventura, CA?
The cost of living index in Ventura, CA is 183 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $2,101, median household income is $100,407/year, and the median home value is $778,700.
Is Ventura, CA affordable?
No, Ventura is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 183. The rent-to-income ratio is 25.1%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
