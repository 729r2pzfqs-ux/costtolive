Cost of Living Overview

Fort Lauderdale has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 132 (where 100 equals the national median), Fort Lauderdale residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,776/month while earning a median household income of $79,935/year.

The median home value in Fort Lauderdale, FL is $455,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,778 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.7%, Fort Lauderdale falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Fort Lauderdale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Fort Lauderdale

What is the cost of living in Fort Lauderdale, FL?
The cost of living index in Fort Lauderdale, FL is 132 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,776, median household income is $79,935/year, and the median home value is $455,600.
Is Fort Lauderdale, FL affordable?
No, Fort Lauderdale is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 132. The rent-to-income ratio is 26.7%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Fort Lauderdale, FL?
The median gross rent in Fort Lauderdale, FL is $1,776 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $21,312.
What is the median household income in Fort Lauderdale?
The median household income in Fort Lauderdale, FL is $79,935 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Florida?
Florida has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.0%, and an effective property tax rate of 0.86%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
