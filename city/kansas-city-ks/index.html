Cost of Living Overview

Kansas City is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 65 (where 100 equals the national median), Kansas City residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,073/month while earning a median household income of $59,183/year.

The median home value in Kansas City, KS is $147,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,054 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Kansas City has a moderate income tax rate of 5.7%. Combined with a 6.5% sales tax rate and 1.41% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Kansas City.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Kansas City

What is the cost of living in Kansas City, KS?
The cost of living index in Kansas City, KS is 65 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,073, median household income is $59,183/year, and the median home value is $147,100.
Is Kansas City, KS affordable?
Yes, Kansas City is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 65. The rent-to-income ratio is 21.8%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Kansas City, KS?

What is the median household income in Kansas City?
