Cost of Living Overview

Sunnyvale has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 341 (where 100 equals the national median), Sunnyvale residents can expect to pay median rent of $3,065/month while earning a median household income of $181,862/year.

The median home value in Sunnyvale, CA is $1,816,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $3,016 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.2% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Sunnyvale.

Common questions about living costs in Sunnyvale

What is the cost of living in Sunnyvale, CA?
The cost of living index in Sunnyvale, CA is 341 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $3,065, median household income is $181,862/year, and the median home value is $1,816,600.

What is the median household income in Sunnyvale?
