Cost of Living Overview

Evansville is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 57 (where 100 equals the national median), Evansville residents can expect to pay median rent of $949/month while earning a median household income of $52,251/year.

The median home value in Evansville, IN is $129,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $919 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Indiana has a moderate income tax rate of 3.05%. Combined with a 7.0% sales tax rate and 0.83% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Evansville.
