Cost of Living Overview

Abilene is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 71 (where 100 equals the national median), Abilene residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,106/month while earning a median household income of $62,720/year.

The median home value in Abilene, TX is $178,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,114 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.2% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Abilene.

Common questions about living costs in Abilene

What is the cost of living in Abilene, TX?
The cost of living index in Abilene, TX is 71 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,106, median household income is $62,720/year, and the median home value is $178,900.
Is Abilene, TX affordable?
Yes, Abilene is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 71. The rent-to-income ratio is 21.2%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Abilene, TX?

What is the median household income in Abilene?
The median household income in Abilene, TX is $62,720 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Texas?
Texas has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.6%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
