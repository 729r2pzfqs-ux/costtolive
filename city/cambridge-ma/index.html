Cost of Living Overview

Cambridge has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 239 (where 100 equals the national median), Cambridge residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,742/month while earning a median household income of $126,469/year.

The median home value in Cambridge, MA is $1,040,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,634 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.0%, Cambridge falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Massachusetts's top income tax rate of 9.0% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.15% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Cambridge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Cambridge

What is the cost of living in Cambridge, MA?
The cost of living index in Cambridge, MA is 239 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $2,742, median household income is $126,469/year, and the median home value is $1,040,500.
Is Cambridge, MA affordable?
No, Cambridge is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 239. The rent-to-income ratio is 26.0%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.

What is the median household income in Cambridge?
