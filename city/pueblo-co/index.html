Cost of Living Overview

Pueblo is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 72 (where 100 equals the national median), Pueblo residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,020/month while earning a median household income of $55,305/year.

The median home value in Pueblo, CO is $230,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,011 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Colorado has a moderate income tax rate of 4.4%. Combined with a 2.9% sales tax rate and 0.51% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Pueblo.
