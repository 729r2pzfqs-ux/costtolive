Cost of Living Overview

Paradise has a cost of living close to the national average, offering a balanced mix of expenses and amenities. With a cost index of 99 (where 100 equals the national median), Paradise residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,287/month while earning a median household income of $58,874/year.

The median home value in Paradise, NV is $358,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,290 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.2%, Paradise falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Nevada has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.85% sales tax rate and 0.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Paradise.
