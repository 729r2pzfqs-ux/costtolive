Cost of Living Overview

Springfield is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 79 (where 100 equals the national median), Springfield residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,114/month while earning a median household income of $51,339/year.

The median home value in Springfield, MA is $222,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,276 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.0%, Springfield falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Massachusetts's top income tax rate of 9.0% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.15% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Springfield.

What is the cost of living in Springfield, MA?
