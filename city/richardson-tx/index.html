Cost of Living Overview

Richardson has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 128 (where 100 equals the national median), Richardson residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,825/month while earning a median household income of $96,257/year.

The median home value in Richardson, TX is $405,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,796 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Richardson.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Richardson

What is the cost of living in Richardson, TX?
The cost of living index in Richardson, TX is 128 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,825, median household income is $96,257/year, and the median home value is $405,600.
Is Richardson, TX affordable?
No, Richardson is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 128. The rent-to-income ratio is 22.8%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Richardson, TX?
The median gross rent in Richardson, TX is $1,825 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $21,900.
What is the median household income in Richardson?
The median household income in Richardson, TX is $96,257 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Texas?
Texas has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.6%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
