Cost of Living Overview

Huntsville is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 78 (where 100 equals the national median), Huntsville residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,078/month while earning a median household income of $70,778/year.

The median home value in Huntsville, AL is $263,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,046 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 18.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Alabama has a moderate income tax rate of 5.0%. Combined with a 4.0% sales tax rate and 0.41% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Huntsville.
