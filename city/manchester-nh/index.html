Cost of Living Overview

Manchester is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 106 (where 100 equals the national median), Manchester residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,465/month while earning a median household income of $77,415/year.

The median home value in Manchester, NH is $336,300, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,548 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.7% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

New Hampshire has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 0% sales tax rate and 1.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Manchester.
