Cost of Living Overview

Philadelphia offers a below-average cost of living, giving residents more purchasing power compared to many US cities. With a cost index of 85 (where 100 equals the national median), Philadelphia residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,323/month while earning a median household income of $60,698/year.

The median home value in Philadelphia, PA is $232,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,233 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.2%, Philadelphia falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Pennsylvania has a moderate income tax rate of 3.07%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 1.49% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Philadelphia.

What is the cost of living in Philadelphia, PA?
The cost of living index in Philadelphia, PA is 85 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,323, median household income is $60,698/year, and the median home value is $232,400.
Is Philadelphia, PA affordable?
Yes, Philadelphia is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 85. The rent-to-income ratio is 26.2%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
