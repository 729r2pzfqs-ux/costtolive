Cost of Living Overview

Spring Valley is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 119 (where 100 equals the national median), Spring Valley residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,642/month while earning a median household income of $72,364/year.

The median home value in Spring Valley, NV is $407,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,554 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 27.2%, Spring Valley falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Nevada has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.85% sales tax rate and 0.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Spring Valley.

What is the cost of living in Spring Valley, NV?
