Cost of Living Overview

San Jose has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 254 (where 100 equals the national median), San Jose residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,617/month while earning a median household income of $141,565/year.

The median home value in San Jose, CA is $1,187,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,775 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.2% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in San Jose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in San Jose

What is the cost of living in San Jose, CA?
The cost of living index in San Jose, CA is 254 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $2,617, median household income is $141,565/year, and the median home value is $1,187,800.
Is San Jose, CA affordable?
No, San Jose is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 254. The rent-to-income ratio is 22.2%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in San Jose, CA?
The median gross rent in San Jose, CA is $2,617 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $31,404.
What is the median household income in San Jose?
The median household income in San Jose, CA is $141,565 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in California?
