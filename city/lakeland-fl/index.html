Cost of Living Overview

Lakeland is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 82 (where 100 equals the national median), Lakeland residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,299/month while earning a median household income of $60,947/year.

The median home value in Lakeland, FL is $229,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,149 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.6%, Lakeland falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Florida has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.86% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Lakeland.

What is the cost of living in Lakeland, FL?
