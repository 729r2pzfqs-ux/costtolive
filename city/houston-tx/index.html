Cost of Living Overview

Houston offers a below-average cost of living, giving residents more purchasing power compared to many US cities. With a cost index of 88 (where 100 equals the national median), Houston residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,313/month while earning a median household income of $62,894/year.

The median home value in Houston, TX is $253,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,316 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.1%, Houston falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Houston.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Houston

What is the cost of living in Houston, TX?
The cost of living index in Houston, TX is 88 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,313, median household income is $62,894/year, and the median home value is $253,400.
Is Houston, TX affordable?
Yes, Houston is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 88. The rent-to-income ratio is 25.1%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Houston, TX?
The median gross rent in Houston, TX is $1,313 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $15,756.
What is the median household income in Houston?
The median household income in Houston, TX is $62,894 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Texas?
Texas has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.6%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
