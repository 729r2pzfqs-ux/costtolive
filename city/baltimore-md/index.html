Cost of Living Overview

Baltimore is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 84 (where 100 equals the national median), Baltimore residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,290/month while earning a median household income of $59,623/year.

The median home value in Baltimore, MD is $219,300, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,333 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 26.0%, Baltimore falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Maryland has a moderate income tax rate of 5.75%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 1.07% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Baltimore.

What is the cost of living in Baltimore, MD?
The cost of living index in Baltimore, MD is 84 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,290, median household income is $59,623/year, and the median home value is $219,300.

Yes, Baltimore is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 84. The rent-to-income ratio is 26.0%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
