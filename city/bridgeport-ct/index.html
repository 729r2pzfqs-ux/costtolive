Cost of Living Overview

Bridgeport offers a below-average cost of living, giving residents more purchasing power compared to many US cities. With a cost index of 94 (where 100 equals the national median), Bridgeport residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,405/month while earning a median household income of $56,584/year.

The median home value in Bridgeport, CT is $252,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,475 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 29.8%, Bridgeport falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Connecticut has a moderate income tax rate of 6.99%. Combined with a 6.35% sales tax rate and 2.15% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Bridgeport.
