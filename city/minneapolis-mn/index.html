Cost of Living Overview

Minneapolis has a cost of living close to the national average, offering a balanced mix of expenses and amenities. With a cost index of 102 (where 100 equals the national median), Minneapolis residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,329/month while earning a median household income of $80,269/year.

The median home value in Minneapolis, MN is $345,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,513 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 19.9% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Minnesota's top income tax rate of 9.85% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.875% sales tax rate and 1.11% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Minneapolis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Minneapolis

What is the cost of living in Minneapolis, MN?
The cost of living index in Minneapolis, MN is 102 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,329, median household income is $80,269/year, and the median home value is $345,600.
Is Minneapolis, MN affordable?
No, Minneapolis is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 102. The rent-to-income ratio is 19.9%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Minneapolis, MN?
The median gross rent in Minneapolis, MN is $1,329 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $15,948.
What is the median household income in Minneapolis?
The median household income in Minneapolis, MN is $80,269 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Minnesota?
Minnesota has a top income tax rate of 9.85%, a sales tax of 6.875%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.11%.
