Cost of Living Overview

Fort Wayne is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 63 (where 100 equals the national median), Fort Wayne residents can expect to pay median rent of $959/month while earning a median household income of $60,293/year.

The median home value in Fort Wayne, IN is $169,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $950 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 19.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Indiana has a moderate income tax rate of 3.05%. Combined with a 7.0% sales tax rate and 0.83% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Fort Wayne.
