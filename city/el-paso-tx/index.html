Cost of Living Overview

El Paso is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 67 (where 100 equals the national median), El Paso residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,041/month while earning a median household income of $58,734/year.

The median home value in El Paso, TX is $171,700, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,044 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Texas has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 1.6% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in El Paso.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in El Paso

What is the cost of living in El Paso, TX?
The cost of living index in El Paso, TX is 67 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,041, median household income is $58,734/year, and the median home value is $171,700.
Is El Paso, TX affordable?
Yes, El Paso is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 67. The rent-to-income ratio is 21.3%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in El Paso, TX?
The median gross rent in El Paso, TX is $1,041 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $12,492.
What is the median household income in El Paso?
The median household income in El Paso, TX is $58,734 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in Texas?
Texas has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 6.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.6%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
