Cost of Living Overview

Kent has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 145 (where 100 equals the national median), Kent residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,833/month while earning a median household income of $90,416/year.

The median home value in Kent, WA is $537,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,915 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Washington has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 6.5% sales tax rate and 0.92% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Kent.

Common questions about living costs in Kent

What is the cost of living in Kent, WA?
The cost of living index in Kent, WA is 145 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,833, median household income is $90,416/year, and the median home value is $537,500.
Is Kent, WA affordable?
No, Kent is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 145. The rent-to-income ratio is 24.3%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.

What is the median household income in Kent?

What are the taxes like in Washington?
