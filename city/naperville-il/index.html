Cost of Living Overview

Naperville has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 147 (where 100 equals the national median), Naperville residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,852/month while earning a median household income of $150,937/year.

The median home value in Naperville, IL is $509,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,161 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 14.7% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Illinois has a moderate income tax rate of 4.95%. Combined with a 6.25% sales tax rate and 2.07% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Naperville.
