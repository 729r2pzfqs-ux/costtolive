Cost of Living Overview

Oklahoma City is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 74 (where 100 equals the national median), Oklahoma City residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,083/month while earning a median household income of $66,702/year.

The median home value in Oklahoma City, OK is $215,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,133 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 19.5% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Oklahoma City has a moderate income tax rate of 4.75%. Combined with a 4.5% sales tax rate and 0.9% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Oklahoma City.

What is the cost of living in Oklahoma City, OK?

Is Oklahoma City, OK affordable?
