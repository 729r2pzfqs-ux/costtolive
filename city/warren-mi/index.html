Cost of Living Overview

Warren is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 73 (where 100 equals the national median), Warren residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,184/month while earning a median household income of $63,741/year.

The median home value in Warren, MI is $181,900, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,098 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Michigan has a moderate income tax rate of 4.25%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 1.38% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Warren.
