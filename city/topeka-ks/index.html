Cost of Living Overview

Topeka is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 58 (where 100 equals the national median), Topeka residents can expect to pay median rent of $947/month while earning a median household income of $55,902/year.

The median home value in Topeka, KS is $130,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $930 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 20.3% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Kansas has a moderate income tax rate of 5.7%. Combined with a 6.5% sales tax rate and 1.41% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Topeka.
