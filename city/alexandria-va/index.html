Cost of Living Overview

Alexandria has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 173 (where 100 equals the national median), Alexandria residents can expect to pay median rent of $2,031/month while earning a median household income of $113,638/year.

The median home value in Alexandria, VA is $696,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,173 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.4% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Virginia has a moderate income tax rate of 5.75%. Combined with a 5.3% sales tax rate and 0.8% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Alexandria.
