Cost of Living Overview

Charleston has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 128 (where 100 equals the national median), Charleston residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,632/month while earning a median household income of $90,038/year.

The median home value in Charleston, SC is $469,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,688 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 21.8% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

South Carolina has a moderate income tax rate of 6.4%. Combined with a 6.0% sales tax rate and 0.57% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Charleston.

What is the cost of living in Charleston, SC?
