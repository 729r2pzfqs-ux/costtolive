Cost of Living Overview

Jersey City has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 148 (where 100 equals the national median), Jersey City residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,902/month while earning a median household income of $94,813/year.

The median home value in Jersey City, NJ is $534,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $2,006 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 24.1% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

New Jersey's top income tax rate of 10.75% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.625% sales tax rate and 2.23% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Jersey City.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Jersey City

What is the cost of living in Jersey City, NJ?
The cost of living index in Jersey City, NJ is 148 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,902, median household income is $94,813/year, and the median home value is $534,500.
Is Jersey City, NJ affordable?
No, Jersey City is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 148. The rent-to-income ratio is 24.1%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Jersey City, NJ?
The median gross rent in Jersey City, NJ is $1,902 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $22,824.
What is the median household income in Jersey City?
The median household income in Jersey City, NJ is $94,813 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in New Jersey?
New Jersey has a top income tax rate of 10.75%, a sales tax of 6.625%, and an effective property tax rate of 2.23%.
