Cost of Living Overview

Sioux Falls is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 78 (where 100 equals the national median), Sioux Falls residents can expect to pay median rent of $993/month while earning a median household income of $74,714/year.

The median home value in Sioux Falls, SD is $271,400, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,122 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 15.9% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

South Dakota has no state income tax, which can represent significant savings compared to high-tax states. Combined with a 4.2% sales tax rate and 1.28% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment helps keep costs manageable in Sioux Falls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in Sioux Falls

What is the cost of living in Sioux Falls, SD?
The cost of living index in Sioux Falls, SD is 78 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $993, median household income is $74,714/year, and the median home value is $271,400.
Is Sioux Falls, SD affordable?
Yes, Sioux Falls is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 78. The rent-to-income ratio is 15.9%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in Sioux Falls, SD?
The median gross rent in Sioux Falls, SD is $993 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $11,916.
What is the median household income in Sioux Falls?
The median household income in Sioux Falls, SD is $74,714 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in South Dakota?
South Dakota has a top income tax rate of 0%, a sales tax of 4.2%, and an effective property tax rate of 1.28%. Notably, there is no state income tax.
