Cost of Living Overview

Elizabeth is more expensive than the national average. Higher housing costs are typically the primary driver. With a cost index of 112 (where 100 equals the national median), Elizabeth residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,463/month while earning a median household income of $63,874/year.

The median home value in Elizabeth, NJ is $393,500, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,543 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 27.5%, Elizabeth falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

New Jersey's top income tax rate of 10.75% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 6.625% sales tax rate and 2.23% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Elizabeth.
