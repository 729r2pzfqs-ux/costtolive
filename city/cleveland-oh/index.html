Cost of Living Overview

Cleveland is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 51 (where 100 equals the national median), Cleveland residents can expect to pay median rent of $894/month while earning a median household income of $39,187/year.

The median home value in Cleveland, OH is $94,100, with typical monthly owner costs around $866 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 27.4%, Cleveland falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

Ohio has a moderate income tax rate of 3.5%. Combined with a 5.75% sales tax rate and 1.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Cleveland.

What is the cost of living in Cleveland, OH?
The cost of living index in Cleveland, OH is 51 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $894, median household income is $39,187/year, and the median home value is $94,100.
Is Cleveland, OH affordable?
Yes, Cleveland is more affordable than the national average with a cost index of 51. The rent-to-income ratio is 27.4%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.

What is the median household income in Cleveland?
