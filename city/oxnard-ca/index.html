Cost of Living Overview

Oxnard has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 159 (where 100 equals the national median), Oxnard residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,962/month while earning a median household income of $93,372/year.

The median home value in Oxnard, CA is $617,800, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,976 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.2%, Oxnard falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Oxnard.

What is the cost of living in Oxnard, CA?
The cost of living index in Oxnard, CA is 159 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,962, median household income is $93,372/year, and the median home value is $617,800.
