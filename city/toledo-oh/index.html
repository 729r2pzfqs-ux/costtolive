Cost of Living Overview

Toledo is significantly cheaper than the national average, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious individuals and families. With a cost index of 52 (where 100 equals the national median), Toledo residents can expect to pay median rent of $880/month while earning a median household income of $47,532/year.

The median home value in Toledo, OH is $107,000, with typical monthly owner costs around $856 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. The median rent-to-income ratio of 22.2% means housing is relatively affordable here — well below the 30% threshold that HUD considers cost-burdened.

Ohio has a moderate income tax rate of 3.5%. Combined with a 5.75% sales tax rate and 1.53% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in Toledo.
