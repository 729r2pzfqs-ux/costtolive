Cost of Living Overview

East Los Angeles has a significantly higher cost of living than most US cities, driven largely by premium housing costs. With a cost index of 134 (where 100 equals the national median), East Los Angeles residents can expect to pay median rent of $1,431/month while earning a median household income of $68,378/year.

The median home value in East Los Angeles, CA is $613,600, with typical monthly owner costs around $1,458 including mortgage, insurance, and taxes. With a rent-to-income ratio of 25.1%, East Los Angeles falls within the generally affordable range, though it's approaching the 30% burden threshold.

California's top income tax rate of 13.3% is among the highest in the nation, adding to the overall cost burden. Combined with a 7.25% sales tax rate and 0.71% effective property tax rate, the overall tax environment is a factor in the total cost of living in East Los Angeles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about living costs in East Los Angeles

What is the cost of living in East Los Angeles, CA?
The cost of living index in East Los Angeles, CA is 134 (US average = 100). Median monthly rent is $1,431, median household income is $68,378/year, and the median home value is $613,600.
Is East Los Angeles, CA affordable?
No, East Los Angeles is more expensive than the national average with a cost index of 134. The rent-to-income ratio is 25.1%—below the 30% threshold considered affordable by HUD.
What is the average rent in East Los Angeles, CA?
The median gross rent in East Los Angeles, CA is $1,431 per month according to the US Census Bureau ACS 2023 data. Annual rent costs approximately $17,172.
What is the median household income in East Los Angeles?
The median household income in East Los Angeles, CA is $68,378 per year. This compares to the national median of approximately $80,000.
What are the taxes like in California?
California has a top income tax rate of 13.3%, a sales tax of 7.25%, and an effective property tax rate of 0.71%.
